- Eye Spy
	- Required Parts
		- Feather
		- Motor
		- ESP32-eye
		- Potentiometer
	- The input from the potentiometer will send analog information to the feather which will transmit it to the motor which will turn the ESP32 every direction until you find a certain thing it can recognize and is looking for. The ESP32 will return serial data and the interface will primarily be on the serial monitor.
