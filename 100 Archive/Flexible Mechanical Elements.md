### **Basic - Flat Belts**
- Can be used for long center distances
- There is some slip (insufficient frictional grip betwen pulley and belt) and creep (uneven extensions and contractions of the belt when it passes from tight side to slack side aka there is relative motion between belt and pulley) so angular velocity is not constant.
- Tension adjustment needed due to age (or installation of new belt)
- Idler or tension puley can provide constant adjustment

**Flat Belt - Open Belt Configuration**
- Modern flat-belt drive is made of a strong elastic core surrounded by an elastomer (rubber like material of a polymer)
- Efficiency for a flat belt is about 90% (V-belt efficiency is 70-96%)
- Flat belts produce little noise and absorb more torsional vibration than V-belt or gear drives
