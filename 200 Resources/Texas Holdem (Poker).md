## Vocabulary
- Dealer
	- Every player in the game takes a turn to be the dealer. I think it usually goes left
- Small Blind
	- Always to the left of the dealer. This person gets the bets started
- Big Blind
	- Always to the left of Small Blind. This person must bet double whatever small blind bet
- The Action
	- The next move after dealing and initial bets is done by The Action who is to the left of the Big Blind. This is true for the first play of the game, but The Action is whoever's turn it currently is. They can **Call, Raise, Check, or Fold**
- Call
	- This means to match the previous bet. This tends to be a modest move as you would likely do this if your hand is not the worst, but maybe not a winner
- Raise
	- This means to add money to the pot--specifically by doubling (or more) the previous bet. This tends to be considered an aggressive move. 
- Check
	- This means that you don't commit anymore money to the pot, but also do not get out of the pot. This is only allowed when nobody before you has bet in this round
- Fold
	- This means to sit out of betting for this hand. A player would do this when they find that their hand of cards is not favorable and/or they current bet is too high for them to stay in the game. It is too expensive.

## Game Flow
##### 1. First Round of Betting
1. Initial bets placed by **Small Blind** and **Big Blind**
2. Two cards dealt to each player
3. **The Action** must decide if they will **Call, Raise, or Fold**. Every player takes turn being **The Action**, and once all players have made a decision the round of betting is over
#####  2. The Flop
1. **The Dealer** draws three cards to the table
2. Once the cards are visibly on the table, the players can look at their own cards and decide how they favor in creating the best combination of cards
##### 3. Second Round of Betting
1. Similar to the first round, the person to the left of the dealer is **The Action**. However, since there are already bets on the table the **Small Blind** and **Big Blind** are not assigned. Instead, **The Action** can choose one of the three usual decisions (**Call, Raise, Fold**) or a new option available **Check**. **Check** is only available when no one else in the round has bet. 
2. **The Action** continues to rotate clockwise
##### 4. The Turn
1. The dealer draws a fourth card on to the table, adding to the previous three already laid on the table
##### 5. Third Round of Betting
1. Betting resumes as normal. The players are able to **Check** if nobody else has bet yet
##### 6. The River
1. The dealer draws a fifth card on to the table, adding to the previous four
##### 7. Turn Over Your Cards
1. All of the players turn over their two cards for everybody to see
2. The winning hand wins the pot
3. **The Dealer** becomes the person originally to the left of the dealer (**Small Blind**) and the game can be restarted with new hands dealt and starting with a new flop
