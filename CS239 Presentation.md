### Basis of Investigation
Research has shown that language-capable robots:
1. have significant **persuasive power** and can influence, persuade, and coerce humans in a variety of way
2. can **influence** interactants’ endorsements of **social and moral norms**
3. may have a **long-term impact on interactants’** social and moral behaviors and on the behaviors those interactants choose to condone or sanction in others

Thus, language-capable social robots, they must have the requisite moral competence to avoid damaging their social and moral ecosystem

- Moral core
- Moral cognition
