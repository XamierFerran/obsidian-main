## Sacred Civics Thesis: 
1. Cities are critical sites of societal transformation and civilizational change
2. Holism is foundational to evolving social constructions and upending assumptions in dominant worldviews that currently shape most city building.
	1. Holism: "understanding people and cities as multitudes of interdependent and complex systems, and with attention to spirituality and the sacred" (Engle Pg. 7)
3. Enacting a sacred civics invites an ennobling of city builders and placekeepers to honor higher order responsibilities and accountabilities
