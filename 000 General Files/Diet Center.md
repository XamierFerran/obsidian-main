## What happens if you exercise and don't eat enough protein?
- List of symptoms that are relevant to me:
	- You feel hungry soon after eating
	- Thinning hair
	- Always feel sore
	- Aren't gaining muscle mass
	- Your immune system is compromised
- I have felt all of these at one point or another but these all also assume a consistent workout plan. I have felt these while also having a consistent workout plan so that overall just means I need more protein.
- Source: https://www.muscleandfitness.com/nutrition/gain-mass/9-signs-you-re-not-getting-enough-protein/
## How to get a more protein filled diet?
- Snack on cheese. 1 oz of cheddar cheese contains 7g of protein. Pair with grain crackers, or tomatoes, or grapes, or sliced aples
- Eat more eggs
- Add chopped almonds into yogurt
- Choose greek yogurt
- Protein shake for breakfast
- Include a high protein food in every meal
	- meat
	- fish
	- pultry
	- eggs
	- legumes
- Add peanut butter to your diet
- Eat more whole grains
- Eat more legumes and beans
