A multi-dimensional rating scale is proposed in which information about the magnitude and sources of six workload-related factors are combined to derive a sensitive and reliable estimate of workload.

## Introduction
Multi year research to empirically isolate and define factors that re relevant to subjective experiences of workload, and formalize evaluation of workload across a variety of activities.
