## Observations
- Each graph has noticeable spikes. The spikes at the beginning or end could be due to a quick change in speed at the beginning and end of the trial where I had to either start running (increase movement) or stop running (decrease movement)
- Intermediate spikes could be due to slight jerks in certain directions. Note that the spikes in the middle of the graph (walking in-pocket, walking fast in-pocket, and walking in-hand) are primarily in one access
- I would have expected a significant difference in scale between all of the graphs where the phone was in my hands versus when it was in my pocket. That could be due to lack of sensitivity from the accelerometer. This could also be due to lack of consistency in time scales across graphs.
