## I. Introduction: The fragile balance between humans and automation
### In general
Machines can make our lives easier if properly applied, or miserable if improperly applied. This raises the question, how should we design these systems?
### Applied to movement
Between cars and planes, automation has made great strides in taking workload from the human operator. However, in the case of planes this has lead to new problems that leave humans with easier manual problems taken care of and harder conceptual problems to solve. 
## II. Inspiration for ability, authority, control and responsibility in cooperative situations from non-technical life
### In general
Cooperation has historically been great for all living things that work together, but has also led to competition as well. In some ways it can also lead to cooperation with the ultimate goal of competing together.
