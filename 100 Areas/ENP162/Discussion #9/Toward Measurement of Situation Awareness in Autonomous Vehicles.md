- Created Daze to measure situation awareness during simulations or real time driving
- Situ-aware important for transition from auton to manual
- "if you build a system where people rarely required to respond, they will rarely respond when required"
### Endsley's Construct (ref [[Toward a Theory of Situation Awareness in Dynamic Systems]])
- perception of elements in environment 
- comprehension of situation
- projection of future state
### Measuring Awareness
- Eye tracking
- Physiological measurements
- SAGAT freeze frame mid simulation questions
- SART (subjective ranking techniques)
- Question probes
### Design Requirements
Driver in simulation vs. IRL are different so testing situational awareness across both is useful for comparison
### Design Model
- uses waze interface because demonstrates Endsley's model
