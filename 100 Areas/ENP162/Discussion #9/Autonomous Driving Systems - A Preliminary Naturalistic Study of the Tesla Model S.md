## The Challenge for Operators of Autonomous Systems
Prior research shows that situational awareness (SA) is necessary for the transition from fully or partially autonomous to manual control of a driver. There are three main factors that have a large effect on driver SA with autonomous systems:
- Attention and Trust
- Engagement and Workload
- Mental Model
