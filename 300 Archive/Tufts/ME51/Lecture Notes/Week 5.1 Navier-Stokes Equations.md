**Background reasoning for this new set of equations**
When we describe viscous flows we have to include viscous forces in our governing equations. Because it depends on very local quantities like the velocity gradient it makes more sense to rep mom and mass consv in a point wise form instead of an integral form. Thus, Navier-aStokes Equation. Before, all we were really doing was the inlet and outlet terms.
