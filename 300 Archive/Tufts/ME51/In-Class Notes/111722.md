Turbulent BLs
- Even if a surface is really smooth, a laminar flow will gain instability and eventually become turbulent 
- Laminar flow builds a thicker BL of constant temperature heat over time. This layer doesn't allow for more heat to come through. Turbulent is more random and cuts through this layer often so that allows for more heat to get to the surface.
